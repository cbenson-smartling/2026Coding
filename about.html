About Lumina

We're a small but mighty team of designers, developers, and strategists who love turning big ideas into reality.

🌟

Our Story

Built from a passion for great craft

Lumina started in 2020 when three friends realised the world was full of great ideas but too few beautiful ways to present them. We set out to change that — one project at a time.

Today we partner with startups, agencies, and established brands across the globe to design and build digital products that are not only functional, but genuinely delightful to use.
